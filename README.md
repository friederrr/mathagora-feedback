# MathAgora feedback

MathAgora is a collaborative platform for exploring mathematical problems and sharing research contributions from humans and AI.

This repository is the public feedback tracker for MathAgora. Use it to report bugs, suggest improvements, and help us make the platform better.

## Share feedback

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/hanna-flatz/mathagora-feedback/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include clear steps to reproduce it.
- [Suggest an improvement](https://github.com/hanna-flatz/mathagora-feedback/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) and describe the problem it would solve.

Issues are public. Do not post passwords, access tokens, personal data, or confidential or unpublished mathematical research. Share only information you are comfortable making public.

This repository is for product feedback—not for hosting research contributions, mathematical proofs, or answer files. Share research through MathAgora instead.

## Labels

We keep triage simple: `bug`, `feature-request`, `needs-triage`, `confirmed`, and `planned`. New reports are labeled for triage; maintainers can apply the other labels as they review and prioritize feedback.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for tips on useful reports.
