# Contributing feedback

Thanks for helping improve MathAgora. Open an issue with the matching bug-report or feature-request form. Search existing issues first, then give a concise title and enough context for someone else to understand the problem or idea.

For bugs, include reproducible steps, what you expected, what happened, and your browser/operating system. Screenshots are helpful when they do not expose private information. For suggestions, explain the problem and the benefit of the proposed improvement.

Issues are public. Never include passwords, access tokens, personal data, or confidential/unpublished mathematical research. Do not attach research proofs or answer files here; use MathAgora for research contributions.

Maintainers will review reports and use labels to organize triage. No response-time commitment is implied.
